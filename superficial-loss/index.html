What the calculator assumes

  • Same coin is identical property everywhere. BTC on one exchange and BTC in your own wallet are identical, so a rebuy anywhere counts (IT-387R2).
  • Moving coins between your own wallets is not a purchase. Leave transfers out of the purchases list.
  • People affiliated with you count too. A rebuy by your spouse or common-law partner, or by a corporation you control, triggers the rule (ITA s.251.1). The calculator counts only the purchases you enter.
  • The window is inclusive. A purchase exactly 30 days before or after the sale date is inside it. Dates are calendar days.
  • Rounding follows CRA's formula as written: the fraction to 8 decimals, then the denied amount. The result matches our tax engine digit for digit on 312 test cases, including CRA's own example.
